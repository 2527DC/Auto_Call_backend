Testing Guidelines

  • Make sure your backend server is running (using nodemon or npm run dev).
  • Click the blue "Initialize & Load Widget" button above.
  • Look at the bottom right corner of this page: a beautiful blue phone icon will appear!
  • Click the phone icon to launch the chat screen and permit microphone access when prompted.
  • Speak into your microphone to have a real-time, ultra-low-latency voice conversation with your custom AI agent!
